Start your map

Three steps, in any order.

  1. Open the aerial map fileThe KMZ holds the stitched aerial image, the property boundary and ground elevations.
  2. Add a layer for each kind of thingWater lines, power lines, existing structures, future structures.
  3. Draw, or import recorded linesKML, KMZ or GeoJSON exports from the GPS receiver land as their own layers.

Select: click anything for its details, drag to move it, right-click for more. Hold Shift and drag a box to select several. Drag the map to pan.
